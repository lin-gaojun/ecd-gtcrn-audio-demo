Setup · Samples & sources

Common setup

Two microphones, 4 cm apart along the x axis. SC uses only the left reference microphone. RIRs are truncated before the peak prior to convolution; scores use the 50 ms early-reverberant speech reference at the reference microphone.

Random tests: 500 samples per angle bin across five bins, plus 1,000 separate samples for the independent average. Fixed scans: a 5 × 5 × 3 m room, target RT60 of 0.20 s and 200 samples per angle. Noise stays at +x (right) or +y (above in plan view); speech moves along a 1 m radius arc.

Samples & display

All seven tracks retain their original amplitude. Spectrograms use a 512-point Hann window and a 128-sample hop. Every track shares the Clean spectral peak as its reference, over −80 to 0 dB.
